\section{Conclusion}

\method{} frames reward construction as a testable tool-use process that produces reusable executable programs. The revised methodology combines a fixed reward-model API catalogue, code-based verification, development-feedback repair, and a lightweight artifact library. It separates reward quality from executability and specifies comparisons against single-completion design, additional sampling, and direct oracle rewards. Construction traces provide a concrete basis for studying Qwen3-8B distillation. The historical experiments retained in this draft motivate the revision but do not establish the effectiveness of the new construction or learning protocols; those claims require the proposed evaluations.
